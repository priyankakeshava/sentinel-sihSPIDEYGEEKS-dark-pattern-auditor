# 3-Minute SIH Demo Script

**Opening (20 sec)**  
“Sentinel is a government auditor that behaves like a consumer. It does not just inspect one webpage; it reproduces the complete journey and collects evidence.”

**Step 1 — Start audit (20 sec)**  
Click **Run Manipulative Audit**.

**Step 2 — Journey (35 sec)**  
Show Search → Product → Cart → Review → Checkout. Explain that every step has a screenshot and captured state.

**Step 3 — Evidence Graph (25 sec)**  
Point out that the journey is connected, so a finding can be tied back to the exact step where the state changed.

**Step 4 — Replay (35 sec)**  
Show the timer decreasing in Session 1 and resetting in a fresh browser session. Explain that Sentinel verifies suspicious urgency behaviour instead of guessing from the text.

**Step 5 — Findings (35 sec)**  
Open Basket Sneaking and Drip Pricing. Point to checkbox state, cart contents, review total and checkout total.

**Step 6 — Human review (15 sec)**  
Click Confirm. “Sentinel does not issue a legal verdict. It prepares structured evidence for the investigator.”

**Step 7 — Clean control (25 sec)**  
Run Clean Control. Show that the same journey can be tested without manipulative behaviour.

**Closing (10 sec)**  
“Our innovation is the combination of journey-level auditing, objective state detection, behavioural replay and evidence-first investigation.”
